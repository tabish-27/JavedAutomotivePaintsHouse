Our Story

Built on Trust,
Driven by Quality

In 1995, Javed Auto Paint House opened its doors in Mukhtar Nagar, Kunda — with a simple promise: the best automotive paints at honest prices, backed by genuine expertise.

Over three decades, that promise has never wavered. We've grown from a small shop to the most trusted automotive paint destination in Pratapgarh district — stocking premium brands like Esdee, Aspa, and offering computerized color matching that was once only available in big cities.

Today, professional painters, auto body shops, and vehicle owners from across the region trust us to deliver quality paint, the right materials, and expert advice — every single time.
